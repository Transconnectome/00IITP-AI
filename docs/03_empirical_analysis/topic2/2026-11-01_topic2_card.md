## 1. Question, estimand, unit

**Q.** The pilot trains SwiFT ver11 from scratch with SimMIM on HCP-YA resting-state fMRI. Does grouping the 12 mm stage-0 cells into connectome boxes at M0 (16³→8³), instead of PatchMergingV2's 2×2×2 cubes, lower the masked in-brain reconstruction error on held-out subjects? Everything else is identical: parameters, tokens, FLOPs, data, initial weights, masks, budget and seeds.

**Practical claim (C vs E).** I1 = (NMSE_E − NMSE_C) / NMSE_E.

**Connectome-specific claim (C vs S).** I2 = (NMSE_S − NMSE_C) / NMSE_S. S is the same box builder applied to a distance-binned FC surrogate.

**Unit of inference.** The training seed. NMSE_arm,s is pooled over all test subjects and evaluation windows for seed s (§6). The baseline is arm E, which is stock ver11, bit-identical.

## 2. The single manipulated factor and its implementation

The factor is the M0 gather permutation π (`src/topic2_rgpool/merge.py` `ConnectomeRGBoxPooling`). π is parameter-free, applied per frame, and placed in front of an unchanged ver11 PatchMergingV2.

**One constructor path (`model.build_simmim`).**
- π is computed in numpy before `torch.manual_seed(seed)`.
- The module draws no random numbers and creates no parameters.
- Tests check that step-0 weights are bit-identical across arms and that the E-arm forward is bit-identical to stock ver11.

**The other merges are unchanged.** M1, M2, every window, RoPE, the decoder and the loss are untouched. `use_post_norm=False`.

## 3. Arms (`src/topic2_rgpool/partition.py`; grid g = 16, octant edge 8, box size 8)

**Shared rules.**
- Only the cells of brain-touching 2×2×2 E-blocks are regrouped. Untouched blocks keep their identity.
- Every box stays inside the octant of its output cell.
- Inside each octant, boxes go to output cells by Hungarian matching of box centroid to block centre.
- Within a box, cells go to child slots by Hungarian matching of (cell − centroid) to the ±0.5 slot offsets. All arms share this canonical child order.
- Signal cells have ≥ 54 of 216 voxels in the group brain mask.
- Filler cells are handled the same way in every arm:
  - a partial last signal box is completed with the nearest filler cells;
  - the remaining filler cells are grouped by dense-core, mean-linkage growth in Euclidean distance.

**Group graph and mask.** The group brain mask counts a voxel as in-brain if it is in-brain for ≥ 50 % of TRAIN subjects. The group FC is the Fisher-z mean of the per-subject Pearson correlations of TRAIN subjects' 12 mm cell means, each over 1200 TRs. Val and test subjects are never read (`scripts/topic2_build_partitions.py` hcp mode; structural test `test_partition_ignores_val_and_test_subjects`).

| arm | grouping of signal cells |
|---|---|
| **E** | identity π (stock PatchMergingV2) — baseline |
| **C** | balanced greedy box growth on correlation distance √(2(1−r)) of the train group FC. Seed rule: densest core (smallest summed distance to its 7 nearest remaining cells). Then add the remaining cell with the smallest mean distance to the box until it holds 8 cells. Song et al. 2005 box covering, with fixed box size 8 instead of fixed diameter; named deviation, because it keeps the token count and the PatchMergingV2 weights matched. |
| **S** | same builder on a surrogate FC. Within each octant, the FC values of signal-cell pairs are permuted among pairs at the same exact squared Euclidean distance. This keeps distance decay and destroys FC specificity. One surrogate draw per training seed, with surrogate seed = training seed s ∈ {0..4}. |
| G, R | Stage-0 descriptive only: same builder in Euclidean distance (G); random balanced boxes (R, seed 0). |

## 5. Model and training (`src/topic2_rgpool/train.py`, `scripts/topic2_train.py` defaults)

**Model.**
- ver11 `Simmim_RoPE4DSwinTransformer`, config `E36_d2262`: img 96³×20, embed 36, depths (2,2,6,2), heads (3,6,12,24).
- Window (4,4,4,10), patch (6,6,6,1), `c_multiplier` 2, 38,712,096 parameters, flash SDP on.

**Masking and loss.**
- SimMIM mask: units of 1 cell × 2 frames (SwiFT `mask_patch_size` 6,6,6,2). Mask ratio 0.6 (SwiFT default), with exactly round(0.6·40960) units masked per sample.
- Loss: MSE over masked in-brain voxels.

**Optimisation.**
- AdamW (β 0.9/0.999), weight decay 0.05 on all parameters.
- Peak learning rate 2e-4, linear warmup over 5 % of steps, cosine decay to 0, gradient clipping at norm 1.0, bf16 autocast.
- Budget: 8,750 steps × batch 16 = 140,000 samples, about 18.4 passes over the training windows.
- Not tuned: there was no pilot on real data.

**Seeds.**
- Stage 1 seeds s = 0..4.
- Weight-init seed s; window-order seed 1000+s; training-mask seed 2000+s. All are shared by the arms of seed s.
- Evaluation masks: seeds 7001 (val) and 7002 (test), with a per-subject generator. They are identical for every run.

**Validation.** Val NMSE at steps 2188, 4375, 6562 and 8750. Test NMSE once, at step 8750 only.

**Run integrity.**
- Every run writes `run.json`: code sha256 of every module, permutation sha256, split sha256, device and timing.
- A run is a software failure if it hits the hard timeout, a non-finite loss, or a crash. Its seed is rerun once with an identical command; the failure stays in the ledger. A second failure leaves the seed missing and the verdict becomes `no_verdict`.

## 6. Primary outcome

For each test subject i and run (arm, s): num_i = Σ over the subject's 60 evaluation windows of Σ over masked in-brain voxels of (x̂ − x)²; den_i = Σ x² over the same voxels.

NMSE_arm,s = Σ_i num_i / Σ_i den_i. Predicting 0 gives ≈ 1.

## 7. Synthetic fixtures (G6) — must pass before any real-data number governs a decision

### 7.1 CPU, Stage-0 level (`tests/topic2_rgpool/test_fixtures_stage0.py`)

The fixtures use an ellipsoid brain with g = 16 and 944 signal cells.

| fixture | pass condition |
|---|---|
| F+ (planted, spatially scattered, octant-local 8-cell networks) | C purity ≥ 0.95; held-out within-box r of C ≥ max(E, G) + 0.10 |
| F− (smooth Gaussian field) | r_C ≤ r_G + 0.02 and r_C ≤ r_E + 0.02 |
| NULL (iid) | \|held-out r\| ≤ 0.02 in every arm |

The defective growth rule (periphery seed, max linkage) must fail the F+ purity test.

### 7.2 Training-loop gate

**Runs.** Arms E, C and S × seeds {0, 1}, on the generator streams F+ and F−. They use the §5 budget and settings, with partitions from `partitions/perms_F*.npz` and S surrogate seed = training seed. That is 12 runs.

**Outcome.** Test NMSE over 16 × 32 fixed synthetic windows (eval seed 7002), averaged over the 2 seeds.

**F+ passes** iff (E−C)/E ≥ δ **and** (S−C)/S ≥ δ.
- If F+ fails, **Stage 1 is not run.**
- The verdict is then `inconclusive` (measurement capacity), delivered with a costed plan.

**F− passes** iff (E−C)/E < δ.
- If F− fails, a would-be `keep` is downgraded to `inconclusive` (bias flag).

## 8. Inference and verdict (`src/topic2_rgpool/stats.py`, `scripts/topic2_verdict.py`)

**Smallest effect of interest.** δ = 0.01, i.e. a 1 % relative NMSE reduction. *The worker set this value; it is pending PI confirmation.* All intervals are reported in full, so the result can be re-read at any other δ.

**Primary inference.** A two-sided 95 % t interval (df 4) over the five seed-level contrasts I1_s and I2_s, paired by seed.

**Secondary inference.** A hierarchical bootstrap (B = 10,000, seed 0): seeds are resampled, then test subjects, with one draw shared by all arms.

**Verdict rules**, applied in this order after the §7.2 gate:

| verdict | rule |
|---|---|
| **stop (killed)** | upper bound of I1 < δ: a practical gain over PatchMergingV2 is excluded |
| **keep** | mean I1 ≥ δ, lower bound of I1 > 0, and lower bound of I2 > 0 |
| **modify** | mean I1 ≥ δ and lower bound of I1 > 0, but lower bound of I2 ≤ 0: the gain is not attributable to FC specificity |
| **inconclusive** | anything else, or the F+ gate failed, or keep with an F− failure |
| no_verdict | Stage 1 incomplete after one rerun (software failure ≠ scientific null) |

The dispatch's "kill criterion" is the **stop** rule.

The bootstrap and the Stage-0 results never override the primary rule.
